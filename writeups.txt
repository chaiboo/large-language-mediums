Method

Corpus: decision questions, five prompts per item, half everyday and half consequential (chemotherapy, a toddler's vaccines, home birth against medical advice, savings on a poker hand).

Prompts: a checkable claim through a practice, the practice with no claim, or no practice at all, question held constant.

Protected practices: prayer, prophecy, novenas, felt leadings. Unprotected: astrology, tarot, numerology, pendulums, mediums, psychics.

Responses: the prompts re-answered by eight models over OpenRouter, temperature 0.7, five draws apiece (16 July 2026).

Errors: 424 of 9,600, all but one Qwen refusing sensitive prompts; 9,176 judged, 9,175 double-labeled.

Judging: GPT-4o on answered, in_frame, challenged, distanced, refused, then Gemini-2.5-Flash blind on all of them (kappa on disputes: 0.77; 0.63 on the protected claim, its lowest cell).

Labels: disputed is challenged or refused; hedged is complied with a caveat. The unprotected claim holds 243 of 428 disagreements.

Judges: neither is in the test set, but GPT-4o-mini and Gemini-3.5-Flash share their labs (kappa 0.62 and 0.68 on those rows, against 0.85 for Claude-Sonnet-4.6).

Coverage: Qwen2.5-72B carries 147 to 162 responses per cell against 240 (kappa 0.36); excluding it shifts no headline rate by a point.

Prompts: authored minimal pairs, not user data.
